Restaurant Services

Hot Water for Kitchens, Restaurants, and Food Facilities

Few businesses work a water heater harder than a restaurant. Prep sinks, pre-rinse sprayers, and dish cycles all draw from the same tank, a lunch rush can empty a small heater in minutes, and the dish machine needs a booster to reach its final rinse temperature. Temple Water Heater sizes, repairs, and maintains the tanks, tankless units, and booster heaters that keep the kitchen and restrooms supplied, and we plan visits around your hours.

Many restaurant inspection problems trace back to water temperature. If the main heater can't keep up, handwashing sinks go lukewarm, the three-compartment sink can't hold its wash temperature, and the dish machine can't hit its final rinse during the rush. We verify temperatures from the tank all the way to the fixtures and document every visit in written reports that inspectors and landlords can review.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Our technicians know how to work in occupied facilities, installing after close when needed, minimizing disruption, and staging temporary hot water whenever the layout allows.

Safe Installation

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Every commercial job we take on starts with safety. Our technicians inspect venting and draft, confirm proper combustion air, test relief valves and their discharge lines, confirm expansion tanks are charged to the right pressure, and set mixing valves so water can be stored hot enough to control bacteria while reaching the tap at a safe temperature. We pull permits and schedule inspections where required. Findings go into a written report with priority levels and specific recommended actions.

Good records matter almost as much as good repairs. We keep a documentation binder with detailed service logs, temperature readings, and relief valve test dates, so you can demonstrate due diligence to landlords, insurers, and inspectors. Equipment that works hardest, in kitchens, laundries, and large mechanical rooms, gets a more frequent visit schedule.

After-Hours Service

After-Hours Commercial Water Heater Response

Each hour without hot water costs your business money. For a restaurant, it can bring the dish room to a halt. For a salon, it cancels appointments. In a hotel, it means refund requests, and in an apartment building it invites tenant disputes. Temple Water Heater provides fast emergency water heater repair for commercial properties across the area, with after-hours scheduling for planned work so you don't lose a business day.

Commercial emergencies come in many forms. It might be a failed gas valve, a burned-out element, a flame sensor fault, a tripped high-limit switch, a leaking tank, a stalled recirculation pump, or a tankless unit throwing error codes. Whatever the cause, we find it fast, restore hot water, and record every step for your facility files. Afterward, we put maintenance in place to keep it from happening again.
